About Me

My journey in cybersecurity and technology

Hello! I'm Nahom Yifru

I'm a cybersecurity professional passionate about building secure and resilient systems. Currently pursuing my B.S. in Computer Science with a focus on Cybersecurity & Information Assurance at Fairleigh Dickinson University (Class of 2027).

My journey into cybersecurity began with a fascination for how systems work and how to protect them from threats. I earned my Associate of Applied Science in Cybersecurity from Bergen Community College with a 3.6 GPA, where I developed a strong foundation in systems administration, network fundamentals, and incident response.

What drives me is the intersection of practical security implementation and continuous learning. Whether it's hardening Active Directory environments, configuring secure network baselines, or exploring digital forensics techniques, I approach each project with curiosity and attention to detail.

My Approach

🔒

Security First

I believe security should be built into systems from the ground up, not bolted on as an afterthought. Every configuration, every policy, every line of code is an opportunity to enhance security posture.

📚

Continuous Learning

Cybersecurity is constantly evolving. I stay current through hands-on labs, certifications, and active participation in the security community. There's always something new to learn.

📝

Documentation Matters

Clear documentation is critical for security and operations. I document baselines, change controls, and procedures to ensure knowledge sharing and accountability.

🤝

Practical Solutions

Security must balance protection with usability. I focus on implementing practical, effective solutions that strengthen defenses while supporting business operations.
